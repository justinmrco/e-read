# Asset and edition credits

Book cover artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. Locally stored covers and cover-extracted decorative cutouts are third-party artwork; source links are references, not evidence of redistribution permission. Permission has not been verified. No source-code license grant should be interpreted as granting rights to this artwork.

The logo was supplied with the project; its authorship and redistribution permission have not been independently established. The public reader contains the project's original sample, not the commercial books. Promotional hooks are original demo copy, not quotations. Prices are sandbox values.

## Other assets

The twelve PNG files in frontend/public/covers/decorations/ are derived from the corresponding covers and inherit the same unresolved artwork permissions. The local sample-cover.svg is the shared fallback. Font families are Cal Sans, Bricolage Grotesque and Cormorant Garamond, loaded through Google Fonts; retain their applicable font licenses when redistributing font files. Interface icons use lucide-react; dependency licenses remain with their respective packages.
